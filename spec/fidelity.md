# Fidelity ledger

Track behaviour that is approximated or deliberately changed for Minecraft, as required by [the brief](../docs/GENSHIN_MINECRAFT_BRIEF.md). Status is one of `specified`, `implemented`, `verified`, or `adapted`; do not mark a design implemented or verified without the corresponding code or evidence. Mechanics reference is Genshin 7.1 unless a dated decision re-pins it.

| behaviour | status (`specified` / `implemented` / `verified` / `adapted`) | reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genshin's 60 fps combat frame timing → Minecraft's 20 TPS execution. | implemented | Plain-Java `EventTimeline` orders a min-heap by original frame then insertion sequence; `advanceTo(3 × serverTick)` drains all due callbacks at their original timestamps. One 20 TPS tick is exactly three 60 fps frames. World/visual execution still occurs during the enclosing Minecraft tick, not at independently rendered sub-tick times; residual in-world differences remain unmeasured. |
| World preservation and vanilla interference in the generated development arena. | adapted | Saved-world managed mode disables hunger/starvation/hunger healing, natural regeneration/spawning, fire spread and mob griefing; survival/adventure breaking, placement and farmland trampling are refused, with creative bypass for owner editing. Weather/day-night and vanilla entity damage are unchanged. This is a Minecraft map-preservation adaptation, not a sourced Genshin combat/traversal implementation; see the 2026-10-04 managed-mode decision. |
| Outgoing character talent damage, directional Melt/Vaporize, ordinary transformative damage, DEF/RES/crit/EM, and single-party particle/orb energy. | implemented | `Stats`, `Damage`, `Reaction` and `Energy` use the sourced tables/formulas in `spec/mechanics/{damage,elements}.md`. Character K(L) is tabulated for 1–20 and 90 without interpolation; transformative damage selects its own RES element and cannot crit. This is the rules implementation, not yet Minecraft entity damage or co-op collection/range integration. |
| Ordinary player-applied aura tax, natural decay, non-additive refill including the Pyro exception, directional gauge consumption, EC coexistence, Frozen, and Swirl multiple-aura ordering. | implemented | `AuraState` keeps separate Pyro/Cryo/Electro/Hydro gauges and a separate nonlinear Frozen gauge. Anemo cannot become an aura. Implements the ordinary starter pair table; Overloaded/Superconduct/EC damage is not a new gauge application. Superconduct exposes −40 percentage points Physical RES for 720 frames independently of reaction damage suppression. |
| Application ICD and ordinary reaction damage ICD. | implemented | `ApplicationIcd` keys owner/tag/target/type, has the finite 24-hit standard sequence and timer-only reset semantics, and supports the sourced Amber interval. `ReactionDamageIcd` separates Overloaded/Superconduct and per-element Swirl limits from aura application; removed entities can release their tracked windows. |
| Traveler (Anemo), Amber, Kaeya and Lisa level 1/20 base stats. | adapted | Named constants preserve the published rounded values, explicitly selecting level 20/20 before ascension and omitting progression-dependent Traveler boosts. They are not recovered internal floats or exact live-game golden expectations. |
| Strong 4U aura duration. | adapted | Named duration constants select the current summary's `2.5u + 7` seconds (17s at 4U), retaining the historical 16.8s disagreement from `elements.md`; the test uses the published duration range, not an exact disputed expectation. |
| Ordinary EC event scheduling, final ticks, ownership and hitlag. | adapted | Caller schedules the initial tick and subsequent sourced 60-frame ticks; damaging ticks consume up to 0.4U from each aura, rejected damage consumes none, and the other aura survives. Early expiry ticks obey the sourced 30-frame exclusion window but round expiry up to the first rules frame and resolve damage before expiry removal. Exact cooldown/event ordering is unknown: `Parameters.electroChargedDamageCooldownFrames` selects the configurable approximate 30-frame baseline. No historical Electro-residue bug is presumed. EM/ownership snapshots and hitlag adjustments remain explicit adapter responsibilities; ticks are not emitted as new reaction triggers. |
| Repeat Frozen, nonzero freeze resistance and hidden Cryo consumption. | adapted | Initial formation and quadratic decay use the sourced full gauge and resistance-duration formula. Zero-resistance history uses named summary loss/acceleration/recovery constants, not the conflicting fitted extension ticket. Unspecified repeat refill uses `max(old Frozen, newly formed Frozen)`, not addition; nonzero resistance restarts fresh-only loss-rate history rather than inventing the unknown combined repeat/resistance equation. Cryo-like reactions consume Frozen and underlying Cryo independently and report one reaction with the total consumed gauge. |
| Blunt pre-Shatter consumption and Shatter owner scope. | adapted | `shatter(frame, preShatterGaugeConsumption)` requires the caller's explicit pre-consumption input because the poise conversion is unknown; only surviving Frozen can Shatter, with the sourced 8U removal rather than unconditional clearing. Shatter damage ICD defaults to target-wide scope, with an explicit owner/target scope option because KQM leaves ownership unresolved. The weakly corroborated extra global Superconduct 0.1s limit and attack-dependent Shatter bugs are not assumed. |
| Arbitrary-input Swirl spread, innate/shield/self auras, Geo/Dendro and Lunar/Stellar reactions. | adapted | No fabricated spread interpolation or shield/self-aura rules: local Swirl returns the swirled element for an explicit adapter spread implementation once its complete gauge rule is sourced. Geo/Dendro and conditional Lunar/Stellar mechanics are outside the ordinary starter rules scope. Environmental aura ownership and co-op item collection range are likewise not inferred. |
