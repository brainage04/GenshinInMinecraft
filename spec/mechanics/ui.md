# PC combat HUD: availability, cooldowns and stamina

Reference target: **Genshin Impact Version 7.1**, pinned in the [brief](../../docs/GENSHIN_MINECRAFT_BRIEF.md#fixed-decisions). Researched 2026-10-07 from public community documentation, not extracted assets/code or datamines. Current wiki descriptions and KQM frame research are not fresh measurements in the pinned release. Exact mask geometry, opacity, pulse duration and error sounds are **unknown** where the sources do not establish them.

**Visual evidence limit:** KQM directly supports grayout and the wiki directly supports the gate/error semantics. ## Skill and Burst icons

- [Elemental Skill — Overview](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Elemental_Skill#Overview): E by default on PC, cannot be used during cooldown; hold-skill cooldown starts **after the hold finishes**. Multiple charges are indicated by **small squares on the Skill icon**. Charged attacks can independently gray out E/Q ([KQM](https://library.keqingmains.com/combat-mechanics/frames#normal-and-charged-attack)).
- [Elemental Burst — Overview](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Elemental_Burst#Overview): the bottom-right icon shows remaining cooldown time; it **fills with the character's element color** as Energy is collected. It glows and plays a cue only after **both** cooldown and energy requirements are fulfilled. Off-field ready Bursts have a separate icon next to the portrait/life bar. Energy can refill while cooldown still runs.
- [KQM Skill/Burst frames](https://library.keqingmains.com/combat-mechanics/frames#elemental-skill-and-burst) distinguishes the frame at which the visible cooldown timer starts from cast input. The sources describe the numeric timer and charge/energy behavior more precisely than the shape of the shadow mask.

The mod draws original stepped diamonds with a **clockwise dark circular cooldown sweep plus the existing seconds**, and a separate **element-colored Burst energy ring and bottom-up diamond fill**. Energy, elapsed cooldown and action restrictions are separate: full Energy cannot clear a cooldown sweep; action recovery dims both icons even with no talent cooldown. Gold outlines supply an original ready-glow analogue; an original colored pip next to an off-field row marks a ready Burst. Circle/ring geometry and the placeholder glow are adaptations, not asserted exact Genshin art. Sweep masks are precomputed scanline runs; no textures, per-frame mask allocation or per-pixel trigonometry are needed.

Each authoritative member snapshot includes the **actual cooldown duration selected by that cast**: Traveler tap5s/hold8s, Lisa tap1s/hold16s, and the sourced single durations of the other starter talents. Dividing by the largest possible cooldown or by the first packet's remaining time would lie after a late packet/switch. Before the source cooldown-start frame, the already-existing authoritative ready-frame countdown may include remaining startup; sweep coverage clamps at1. While holding Palm Vortex there is now **no fake8-second cooldown**: its action gate dims E/Q and locks party rows until release, then the actual cooldown begins.

**Charges:** all current starter characters are C0 with one ordinary Skill use. Do not draw fake multiple-charge squares or introduce unused charge state; future multi-charge kits must supply actual available/maximum charges and independent recharge timing ([Charge](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Charge), [Cooldown](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Cooldown#CD_Reduction)).

## Rejected inputs and normal-attack recovery

The [Error table](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Error#Types) describes brief white overlay text in the top half of the screen, not modal interruptions, for ordinary rejected actions. Its switch messages above are explicitly documented. Neither that retrieved table nor Skill documentation establishes a Genshin message **“Skill not ready”**, an exact E-icon flash or a normal-recovery click sound. Do not attribute those to Genshin based on another game's error strings.

**Decision:** only the server's actual rejection triggers feedback. Switch cooldown/block/fallen/traversal use the sourced switch wording where available. Rejected E/Q display a short white icon border and explanatory white text (**“Elemental Skill is on cooldown”**, **“Elemental Burst is on cooldown”**, **“Not enough Energy”**, or recovery unavailable). These talent strings, white border and **16 client ticks/.8 seconds** are named feedback adaptations, not exact sourced error text/timing. The existing state stream carries a rejection sequence/action/reason and immediately sends a rejection even inside its ordinary three-tick throttle; the client never suppresses an intent or assumes rejection from stale cooldown data. The pulse cannot replay on subsequent resource updates. A quiet Minecraft UI click supplies original, non-copied feedback.

[KQM Normal/Charged Attacks](https://library.keqingmains.com/combat-mechanics/frames#normal-and-charged-attack) describes **start, hitmark and recovery animation**, and [Input/Buffer](https://library.keqingmains.com/combat-mechanics/frames#input-and-buffer) describes normal-input buffering, not a PC normal-attack cooldown timer/icon. **[INFERENCE]** ordinary PC normal recovery is communicated through animation, not a dedicated recovery HUD meter; no explicit equivalent was found in the consulted sources. Therefore **no normal countdown, attack-ready bar or fabricated lock icon is added**. Item20 owns the real wind-up/recovery animation. Until then, a server-rejected normal press gets only the quiet click, with **no normal-specific HUD text/flash**. This is an acknowledged temporary usability adaptation; the existing unbuffered-input rule is unchanged, not passed off as Genshin's buffering model.

Charged-attack resource consumption uses the **shared stamina gauge** ([Stamina](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Stamina)). **[INFERENCE]** no per-press numeric cost sticker is part of ordinary PC charged-attack feedback; no such equivalent was established by the consulted documentation. The current costs remain Traveler/Kaeya20, Lisa50, Amber bow0 ([Stamina — Consumption](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Stamina#Stamina_Consumption), [Charged Attack](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Charged_Attack)); no unsupported attack-cost icon is introduced.

## Stamina gauge and dash recheck

[Stamina](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Stamina) directly states: a **yellow meter to the character's right near screen center**, visible while **depleting/recharging**, automatically hidden at full, with visual sections representing100 units. It belongs to the entire party. Starter capacity100 versus upgraded240 matters: an unmodified18-unit dash is **18% of a new account's pool**, but **7.5% of a fully upgraded pool**. Red exhausted coloring/core, the original48-sample270° arc at35GUI pixels right of the crosshair, and full/idle traversal visibility remain documented Minecraft adaptations, not measured Genshin mask/color thresholds. Exact low-stamina flashing/fade timing is unknown.

Freshly retrieved [Sprinting](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Sprinting) explicitly says **18 per ordinary dash** and **18/s continuous sprinting**. Its Sprint section describes holding input **until the player begins sprinting**, distinct from the initial dash. [Stamina](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Stamina#Stamina_Consumption) agrees. The old KQM15-dash/30-regen calculation remains a historical conflict in [stamina.md](stamina.md#explicit-historical-conflicts-and-reliability), not a reason to lower the selected sourced18.

**Implementation audit:** `CombatRuntime.Session.tickMovement` calls `Stamina.dash` once on the grounded moving Sprint edge. An accepted dash sets `sprinting=true`; the subsequent `startSprint` branch is guarded by `!sprinting`, so it **does not charge another18**. `Stamina.advanceTo` begins continuous drain at `max(previousFrame,dashEnd)`, **not during the18-reference-frame dash**. Reprocessing the same held edge/frame cannot spend again. Existing shared server GameTests `sprintDrainsAndStopsAtZero` and `dashCostsStaminaAndDodgesClub` assert100→82 immediately, still82 through dash end, then64 after one full second of held sprint, and no repeated-frame charge. Thus **no dash double-charge/drain defect was found and no stamina number or billing code is changed**. Exact Genshin transition billing is still unknown; excluding drain during this mod's six-tick dash remains the named pre-existing adaptation. Kaeya's20% utility passive is documented but intentionally not granted to the fixed starter yet; this is another known fidelity difference, not a newly proven cause of the owner's observation.
