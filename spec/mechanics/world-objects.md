# Mondstadt world objects, recovery, camps, and landmark placement

Reference target: **Genshin Impact Version 7.1**, as pinned in [the project brief](../../docs/GENSHIN_MINECRAFT_BRIEF.md#fixed-decisions). Scope: Mondstadt's Anemo Statues of The Seven, ordinary permanent Teleport Waypoints, overworld party defeat, and introductory Starfell Valley / Windrise camps. **World Level 0** below means the starting world tier in the [Adventure Rank table](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank#World_Level_and_Ascension), not a requirement that enemies match the party's level.

**Evidence status:** public wiki text, KQM's published observations and linked original testing, official HoYoverse support, and identified walkthrough screenshots. No datamines or game-asset extraction were used. The public wiki's readable API supplied material that its normal web pages refused to serve; reproducible API links are included below. No mechanics were measured in the pinned client. Historical tests are explicitly dated by their tested version; they support current descriptions but do not constitute new pinned-release verification. A value not established by these sources is `unknown`.

**Numeric camp audit (2026-10-09):** [datamine.md](datamine.md#camp-spawns-and-world-level) records the pinned7.1 WorldLevel/Scene/LevelDesign files checked. No identifiable Starfell/Windrise/Mondstadt-gate spawn IDs or WL0 levels were recovered; WorldLevel has noWL0 row and does not establish per-camp offsets. Keep the existingLv8 adaptation and source-qualified composition table below. No assets or spawn scripts were copied.

### Healing reserve and revival

The source/UI term is **Restorative Power**, presented through **Statue's Blessing**. This is the finite healing reserve, separate from each character's HP and separate from the regional offering level.

| Quantity / behavior | Published value or rule | Source / confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reserve scope | Statues share a healing reserve; it is not a fresh independent pool at every destination or every elemental region. In co-op the source assigns the pool to the host's world and shares it among its players. | [Statue's Blessing and Co-Op Mode](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Statue_of_The_Seven#Statue's_Blessing) |
| Reserve capacity | **5,000 HP per unlocked Statue of The Seven or Statue of the New Moon**. The current wiki counts both types. Do not hard-code a historical worldwide cap for this Mondstadt slice. | [Statue's Blessing](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Statue_of_The_Seven#Statue's_Blessing); [readable source](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/api.php?action=parse&page=Statue_of_The_Seven&prop=wikitext&format=json) |
| Reserve regeneration | **1% of maximum reserve every 15 real-time seconds**; full empty-to-full recharge is **25 minutes**. Regeneration continues while paused. | [Statue's Blessing](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Statue_of_The_Seven#Statue's_Blessing); [historical community answer quoting the wiki and reporting empirical agreement](https://gaming.stackexchange.com/questions/395044/what-is-the-rate-of-recharge-for-the-divine-blessing) |
| Automatic recovery configuration | **Auto-recover when nearby** must be enabled, and a player-selected target HP percentage controls how far recovery goes. | Pause menus | Nearby passive healing continues through pause menus, including the Map. | [KQM original test](https://library.keqingmains.com/evidence/general-mechanics/miscellaneous-entries#statue-healing-works-in-pause-menus), tested in [Version 2.5](https://library.keqingmains.com/evidence/general-mechanics/miscellaneous-entries#statue-healing-works-in-pause-menus), with [video](https://youtu.be/oVJpWywNZF0) |
| Locked / undiscovered Statue | Can revive fallen characters but does not provide further ordinary healing before discovery. | Co-op healing | Nearby healing works with Auto-Recover enabled even though normal offering/Blessing interaction is restricted. **Teleporting to Starfell Lake or Windrise is not itself a guaranteed heal.** Neither is listed among Mondstadt's Statues whose arrival points are already close enough for automatic healing in the [current wiki](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Statue_of_The_Seven#Healing_upon_Teleport); the [original KQM test](https://library.keqingmains.com/evidence/general-mechanics/miscellaneous-entries#statue-healing-works-in-pause-menus) likewise distinguishes automatic arrivals from Statues requiring the player to walk closer. The healing operation depends on proximity, settings, and reserve, not on the teleport command granting a full-HP reset.

**Characters outside the deployed party:** the wiki's **Inactive Party Members** section is explicitly marked for cleanup/testing. It reports a daily restoration limit whose magnitude is `unknown` and a “Recovering” state of **1% Max HP per 15 minutes**, itself marked for verification ([source](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Statue_of_The_Seven#Inactive_Party_Members)). Those claims must not be applied to off-field members of the deployed party or silently promoted into pinned-version rules. The precise dormant-roster revival behavior remains `unknown`.

## Entire-party defeat and respawn

**Ordinary solo overworld wipe:** when every current-party member is dead, the Game Over screen offers **Revive**. It is not a compulsory teleport on the first member's death ([Fallen Character](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Fallen_Character#Game_Over_Screen)).

| Property | Behavior / value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Destination | Nearest eligible **unlocked Teleport Waypoint, Statue of The Seven, or domain entrance**, relative to where the **last party member died**. This is not a bed, the last Statue visited, or necessarily the last teleport destination. | [KQM original Health And Revive Mechanics](https://library.keqingmains.com/evidence/general-mechanics/overworld#health-and-revive-mechanics), with [domain clip](https://youtu.be/Kktd971hhGA), [Statue clip](https://youtu.be/sn0KKSx1D84), and [Waypoint clip](https://youtu.be/sjVzLED1lmM); [Waypoint unlock requirement](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Teleport_Waypoint#Overview) |

Thus **“after a day” is not a universal exact timer**: it may refer to daily-reset groups or imprecise farming advice. Neither a fixed wait nor a Minecraft distance rule should silently replace the unresolved timer origin. The [Reset page itself](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/api.php?action=parse&page=Reset&prop=wikitext&section=0&format=json) flags its timer information for verification/citations.

### Camp chests

- An **opened overworld loot chest does not respawn**, including when camp guards return. Increasing Adventure Rank or World Level does not regenerate opened chests ([Reset — Chests](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Reset#Chests); [KQM Newbie Guide](https://keqingmains.com/misc/newbie-guide/#exploring-overworld-content)).
- An **unopened** chest unlocked by defeating **respawning** guards can become locked again when those guards respawn. By contrast, a chest unlocked by one-time approach-spawned enemies or another one-time mechanism stays unlocked ([Chest — Additional Information](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Chest#Additional_Information); [readable section](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/api.php?action=parse&page=Chest&prop=wikitext&section=13&format=json)).
- Some quests/commissions temporarily hide an existing unclaimed chest. Its reappearance is not a new copy of an already-claimed reward ([same source](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Chest#Additional_Information)).
- The introductory **Going Upon the Breeze** camp rewards an **Exquisite Chest**, not a Common Chest ([quest steps](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Going_Upon_the_Breeze#Steps); [Game8 walkthrough](https://game8.co/games/Genshin-Impact/archives/405608)). Camp encounter type alone is not enough to choose chest grade.
